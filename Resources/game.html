How to Play

1. Drag back from the cue ball (white) and release to strike — the further you drag, the more power.

2. While reds remain: pot a red (1 pt), then any colour (2–7 pts), then a red again, alternating.

3. Colours are re-spotted on their marks after being potted, until the reds run out.

4. Once all reds are gone, colours must be potted in order: yellow, green, brown, blue, pink, black — and stay potted.

5. A foul (wrong ball hit first, no ball hit, cue ball potted, or potting out of order) ends your break and gives the turn to your opponent.

6. Highest score when the black is potted (or table is cleared) wins the frame.
